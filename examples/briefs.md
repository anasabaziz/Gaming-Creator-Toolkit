# Fictional starter briefs

These are request examples, not creator profiles or evidence of real gameplay. Replace placeholders with your own material after onboarding.

## Highlights

"From my supplied non-commentary recording, select up to three distinct encounters with a visible setup and outcome. Preserve sound. Show candidate timestamps before export." This explicitly requests candidate approval before export.

## Trailer

"Create a 45-second landscape trailer from my recording, covering exploration, a meaningful upgrade and one fight. Keep original audio and chronology. Do not invent labels for uncertain mechanics. Export the MP4 and Shotcut project."

## Portrait

"Reframe this approved trailer to 1080 x 1920. Preserve timing and audio. Use synchronized HUD panels if necessary. Ask before replacing a shot."

## Thumbnail

"Create a 16:9 text-free cover from these two references: the first defines character geometry, the second defines color and illustration style. Do not treat either as a logo request. Save the chosen image and brief."

## Article

"Use these firsthand demo notes to draft a review. Locate authentic screenshots in the supplied recording for each eligible H2. Deliver matching Markdown and HTML, SEO suggestions, local images and a private report of unresolved claims. No publication or disclosure relationship is implied."

## Combined request

"From this recording and my notes, create one trailer and an article. Reuse observed events and screenshot evidence. The article should describe only my stated experience. No separate highlight exports or portrait version are requested."
